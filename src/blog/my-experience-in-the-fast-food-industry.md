## lessons

> [!note] Hello, World!

- **teaches good work ethic**
  - teaches you how to take initiative
  - how to handle the pressure
  - how to talk to customers (I learned a bit of spanish along the way)
- **build connections with people**
  - working with people you like makes it feel better (and how to stay professional even with people you dont like)
  - you might meet your best friend or significant other there
  - you have references and options for whenever you leave
- **you will make mistakes and thats ok**
  - teaches you how to handle a bad situation
  - you learn from it so that you dont reproduce it again
  - teaches you to have empathy like whenever someone else screws up
- **even if you hate the job, work hard for yourself**
  - prove to yourself that you know what youre capable of
  - discipline: doing things you dont like builds your abilities
  - treat the job as a stepping stone to achieving better things

## other points and stuff

- set boundaries and always keep it professional
- people will be rude. dont let it get to your head
- respect yourself. know when its right to walk away
- learn compassion. dont let people downplay your experiences
